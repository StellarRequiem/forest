## Grammar & Clear Communication

## Chemistry

## Cryptology
- One-time pad is information-theoretically secure when keys are truly random and never reused (Shannon, 1949). Key distribution remains the practical weakness.

## Ethics
- Asimov's Three Laws (1942) are a starting point but insufficient for autonomous agents with network access. Modern debate centers on "do no harm" vs capability.
- Principle of least privilege (Saltzer & Schroeder, 1975) applies to AI tools: give only the access needed.

- Risk: supply-chain attacks on weights or dependencies. Mitigation: human sign-off and local verification only.
